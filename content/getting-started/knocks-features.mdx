Knock is product and customer messaging infrastructure. Engineering, product, and growth teams use one platform to send transactional messages, run lifecycle and marketing campaigns, and deliver in-product messaging. Developers integrate Knock through the [API](/api-reference), [SDKs](/developer-tools/sdks), and [CLI](/developer-tools/knock-cli). Non-technical teammates manage content, targeting, sends, and measurement in the Knock dashboard.

For an introduction to the concepts behind these features, start with [What is Knock?](/getting-started/what-is-knock)

## Transactional messaging

At the heart of Knock is the [workflow](/concepts/workflows) engine. Workflows model cross-channel messaging logic while respecting each user's preferences:

- **Cross-channel orchestration.** Send to email, SMS, push, chat (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, WhatsApp), and in-app from one workflow. Use [step conditions](/designing-workflows/step-conditions) to sequence delivery, for example to send an email only if the recipient hasn't seen the in-app message.
- **Function steps.** [Batch](/designing-workflows/batch-function) activity into digests, [delay](/designing-workflows/delay-function), [throttle](/designing-workflows/throttle-function), [branch](/designing-workflows/branch-function) on data, [wait for an event](/designing-workflows/wait-for-event-function), [fetch](/designing-workflows/fetch-function) external data, or run an [agent function](/designing-workflows/ai-agent-function).
- **Flexible triggers.** Trigger workflows from the API and SDKs, from a [source](/integrations/sources/overview) such as Segment, on a [schedule](/concepts/schedules), or when a user [enters an audience](/send-notifications/triggering-workflows/audiences).
- **Bring your own providers.** Send through the providers you already use, such as SendGrid, Postmark, Amazon SES, Twilio, APNs, and Firebase Cloud Messaging.
- **Preferences.** Give users a [hosted](/preferences/hosted-preference-center) or [custom](/preferences/custom-preference-center) preference center. Knock enforces their [preferences](/concepts/preferences) on every workflow run and broadcast.
- **Multi-tenancy.** Apply per-tenant branding, translations, and preferences for B2B products with [tenants](/multi-tenancy/overview).

## Lifecycle and marketing messaging

Workflows and broadcasts handle lifecycle and marketing messaging alongside transactional messaging, so every message shares the same templates, preferences, and recipient data.

- **Lifecycle workflows.** Build onboarding sequences, engagement campaigns, and retention flows. Start them when a user enters an audience, and use [wait for event](/designing-workflows/wait-for-event-function) and [branch](/designing-workflows/branch-function) steps to react to what users do.
- **[Broadcasts](/concepts/broadcasts).** Send one-time messages, such as product announcements, marketing campaigns, and newsletters, from the dashboard to an [audience](/concepts/audiences), to all users, or to users from a [CSV upload](/concepts/broadcasts#targeting-users-for-a-broadcast). A broadcast can include [several steps](/concepts/broadcasts#creating-and-managing-broadcast-content), for example an in-app message, a [delay](/designing-workflows/delay-function), and an email follow-up for users who didn't engage. [Schedule a send](/concepts/broadcasts#scheduling-broadcasts) for later, [personalize by tenant](/concepts/audiences#audiences-and-tenants), and run [experiments](/designing-workflows/experiment-function) inside the broadcast.
- **[Audiences](/concepts/audiences).** Sync [static audiences](/concepts/audiences#static-audiences) from a reverse ETL tool such as [Hightouch](/integrations/sources/hightouch) or [Census](/integrations/sources/census), or populate them from a CSV upload, the [API](/api-reference/audiences), or the dashboard. Define [dynamic audiences](/concepts/audiences#dynamic-audiences) with [query rules](/concepts/conditions) on user properties, and Knock updates their membership in real time. [Workflows](/send-notifications/triggering-workflows/audiences), broadcasts, and [guides](/in-app-ui/guides/create-guides#targeting) share the same audiences.
- **Personalize templates.** Edit messages in the drag-and-drop [template editor](/template-editor/overview) with Liquid, [partials](/template-editor/partials/overview), branding, and [translations](/template-editor/translations), and ship copy changes without a code change.
- **Compliance.** [Commercial unsubscribe](/preferences/commercial-unsubscribe) adds a one-click unsubscribe header and link to marketing email to support CAN-SPAM compliance.

## In-product messaging

- **[Guides](/concepts/guides).** Power in-app [banners](/in-app-ui/react/banner), [modals](/in-app-ui/react/modal), paywalls, announcements, and onboarding flows with [your own components](/in-app-ui/guides/render-guides) and design system. Engineering builds the UI once, and content teams manage copy, [targeting](/in-app-ui/guides/create-guides#targeting), and [activation rules](/in-app-ui/guides/create-guides#activation) in the dashboard. Guides react to [runtime data](/in-app-ui/guides/create-guides#personalization-with-runtime-data), so a guide appears only when it's relevant to what the user is doing.
- **In-app feeds and toasts.** Drop in pre-built [feed](/in-app-ui/feeds/overview) and [toast](/in-app-ui/react/toasts) components with real-time delivery, accurate badge counts, and mark-as-read, or build your own UI with the SDKs and API.

## Measurement and optimization

- **Analytics.** The [**Analytics**](/send-notifications/analytics) page shows message volume, delivery, and engagement across workflows, guides, and channels. Each workflow and broadcast has its own **Analytics** tab with per-step metrics, and each guide reports audience, seen, interacted, and archived counts.
- **Goals with attribution.** Define a business outcome, such as "Customer activated", that completes on an integration source event (from Segment, Stripe, or PostHog, for example) or when a user enters or leaves a dynamic audience. Attach the goal to a workflow, broadcast, or guide, and Knock credits each completion to the most recent message within an attribution window you set (1 to 30 days, 7 by default).
- **Experiments.** Add an experiment step to a workflow or broadcast to split recipients into percentage-based cohorts for A/B and holdout testing. Within a step, the same cohort key always lands in the same cohort, so recipients get consistent messaging across runs.
- **Data streaming.** Send message and engagement data to your [data warehouse](/integrations/extensions/data-sync), CDP and analytics tools (Segment, Heap), observability tools (Datadog, New Relic), and [outbound webhooks](/developer-tools/outbound-webhooks/overview). See [extensions](/integrations/extensions/overview) for the full list.

## Team operations and governance

Knock is designed with the needs of teams of all sizes in mind. Logically separated [environments](/version-control/environments), [branches](/version-control/branches), [version control](/version-control/commits), and a full [audit log](/manage-your-account/audit-logs) help you introduce new messaging and manage existing messaging safely. [Roles and permissions](/manage-your-account/roles-and-permissions) control who can edit and send, and Enterprise plans add [SAML SSO](/manage-your-account/saml-sso) and [directory sync (SCIM)](/manage-your-account/directory-sync).

Knock logs every message, including who received it, why, and how they engaged (opens, clicks, reads), for your account's [data retention period](/manage-your-account/data-retention).

## Knock AI

Knock works with the AI tools your team already uses. The [Knock agent](/ai/agent) builds and inspects messaging from the dashboard, and the [Knock MCP server](/ai/mcp-server), [CLI](/ai/cli), and plugins for Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, and Grok Bot connect Knock to your IDE and AI clients. See the [Knock AI overview](/ai/overview) to choose between them.
