Sources bring data from external services into Knock. Use them to trigger workflows and to keep users, tenants, objects, and audiences up to date.

There are two kinds of sources:

- **Event streams.** The service sends Knock an event each time something happens, and you choose what Knock does with each event, such as triggering a workflow or identifying a user. This covers incoming webhooks from services like Stripe, Clerk, and WorkOS, CDPs like Segment and RudderStack, and [custom sources](/integrations/sources/custom) for any other webhook provider. See [event streams](/integrations/sources/streams).
- **Syncs.** A table you maintain elsewhere, such as in your data warehouse, keeps users, tenants, and objects in Knock up to date on a schedule. Connect a warehouse such as Snowflake or Amazon Redshift to Knock directly, or use a reverse ETL tool like Hightouch, Fivetran, or Polytomic, which syncs warehouse data by calling the Knock API. See [syncs](/integrations/sources/syncs).

## Configuring sources

You can configure sources from the **Platform** > **Sources** page in your Knock dashboard. Initial creation of a source is managed at the account level of your Knock account, though you configure specific events and their actions within your Knock environments.

### Per-environment source configuration

If you've enabled [environments](/version-control/environments), each source has a unique configuration for every Knock environment in your account. This makes it possible to connect your development source environment to your Knock development environment. Use the environment dropdown in the top header to switch between environments and view their configurations.
