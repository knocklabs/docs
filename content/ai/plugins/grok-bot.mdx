Knock is a plugin in Grok Bot. Discover it in the Marketplace > Search "Knock".

The plugin runs on the [Knock MCP server](/ai/mcp-server) and can use the [Knock agent](/ai/agent). You do not need a local setup or a service token. Sign in with your Knock account, and Grok Bot picks up tools for the resources in your workspace.
