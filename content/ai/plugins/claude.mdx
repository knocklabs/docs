Knock is a connector in Claude. Add it once, then prompt Claude to build and manage workflows, broadcasts, [guides](/concepts/guides), templates, and audiences from Claude Cowork and Claude Desktop.

The connector runs on the [Knock MCP server](/ai/mcp-server). You do not need a local setup or a service token. Sign in with your Knock account, and Claude picks up tools for the resources in your workspace.

Reading and managing your configuration are enabled by default, along with the [Knock agent](/ai/agent). Debugging, user and tenant data, and documentation search stay off until you turn them on.
