[Connect Claude](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/knock)

Knock is a connector in Claude. Add it once, then prompt Claude to build and manage workflows, broadcasts, [guides](/concepts/guides), templates, and audiences from Claude Cowork and Claude Desktop.

The connector runs on the [Knock MCP server](/ai/mcp-server). You do not need a local setup or a service token. Sign in with your Knock account, and Claude picks up tools for the resources in your workspace.

Reading and managing your configuration are enabled by default, along with the [Knock agent](/ai/agent). Debugging, user and tenant data, and documentation search stay off until you turn them on.

## What you can do

- **Build workflows from a prompt.** Ask for a welcome sequence, and Claude assembles the steps, templates, and conditions.
- **Rework copy across channels.** Point Claude at existing workflows and ask it to tighten subject lines or adapt an email template for SMS and push.
- **Audit what you send.** Ask which workflows have gone quiet, where errors are occurring, or which templates use outdated branding.
- **Plan before you build.** Talk through a lifecycle program, then turn the plan into resources once you are happy with it.
- **Debug a delivery.** Pull message logs for a recipient to see what sent, what rendered, and where a workflow run stopped.

The connector writes to your Knock account, so the result is a resource your team can review, edit, and send.

## Guardrails

- **Changes land in development.** Claude creates versioned resources. Review them in the dashboard, then commit and promote them on your own schedule.
- **Nothing gets deleted.** The MCP server has no delete tools, so an agent cannot remove a workflow, template, or audience.
- **Access follows the signed-in user.** The connector uses that person's Knock permissions.
- **You choose the surface area.** Enable only the capabilities a task needs, and leave the rest off.

## Get started

Open the [Knock listing](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/knock), click Connect, and sign in with your Knock account. Then choose which capabilities to grant.
