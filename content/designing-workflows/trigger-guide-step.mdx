Use a trigger guide step to update a user's [guide](/concepts/guides) eligibility within a workflow or broadcast run. This is useful when eligibility is driven by the context of the workflow or broadcast. For example, recipients may become eligible for an onboarding modal after completing a certain action in a cross-channel onboarding campaign.

## How it works

A trigger guide step references a guide by its key. When you add the step to a workflow or broadcast, Knock creates a managed [static audience](/concepts/audiences) behind the scenes and links it to the guide's targeting rules. When the step executes for a recipient, Knock adds that recipient to the managed audience to make them [eligible for the selected guide](/in-app-ui/guides/create-guides#targeting) .

To receive the rendered guide in your application, eligible audience members must also meet any guide targeting criteria set directly on the guide. This includes [property-based targeting](/in-app-ui/guides/create-guides#targeting) and [activation rules](/in-app-ui/guides/create-guides#activation). Guides may also be subject to [throttling](/in-app-ui/guides/order-guides) depending on how they've been prioritized in against other guides your user is eligible for.

Enrollment is one-directional. A trigger guide step only ever adds the recipient to the managed audience — it never removes them. If a recipient runs through the step more than once, they simply stay enrolled; there's no duplicate membership or error.

## Adding a trigger guide step

1. In the workflow or broadcast builder, drag the **Trigger guide** step onto your canvas. It's listed alongside your channel steps in the step panel.
2. Select the guide you want to make recipients eligible for. Only guides that have been created and [committed](/version-control/commits) in the current environment are available for selection.
3. Optionally, add [step conditions](/designing-workflows/step-conditions) to control when the step should run.

## Guide targeting and the managed audience

When a guide is referenced by a trigger guide step, its **Audience** settings reflect that it's targeted by a workflow or broadcast step rather than, or in addition to, a manually-selected audience. You can see which workflows and broadcasts trigger a given guide in the **Triggered by** section of the guide's details page in the dashboard.

### Tenancy strict mode

If the guide is targeted only through a trigger guide step (no manually-selected audience), you can still enable [tenancy strict mode](/in-app-ui/guides/create-guides#tenancy-strict-mode) on the guide, since the managed audience carries the tenant the recipient was enrolled with.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a trigger guide step in a broadcast?">
    Yes. Trigger guide steps are supported in both workflows and broadcasts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the recipient see the guide immediately?">
    Not necessarily. Enrollment makes the guide eligible, but your application
    still needs to fetch (or refetch) guides for that recipient before the guide
    can render. See [rendering guides](/in-app-ui/guides/render-guides) for how
    to do this in your application.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I remove a recipient's eligibility once they're enrolled?">
    Not through the trigger guide step itself, since it only adds recipients to
    the managed audience. If you need to revoke eligibility, use [property-based
    targeting](/in-app-ui/guides/create-guides#targeting) or [activation
    rules](/in-app-ui/guides/create-guides#activation) on the guide to control
    when it's actually shown, rather than relying solely on audience membership.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I archive or unpublish the guide referenced by a step?">
    The step becomes invalid, in the same way a channel step becomes invalid if
    its channel is removed. Update the step to reference a different committed
    guide, or remove the step.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
